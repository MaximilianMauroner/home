## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Maximilian Mauroner’s personal website contains a blog, development log, and browser tools. The development log records work on personal software projects over time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Astro renders content from Markdown and MDX. Log entries have titles, descriptions, dates, tags, and optional images. Readers can browse the archive, open an entry, follow section links, and visit related entries. Preserve content, routes, accessibility, and metadata when implementing design changes.

## Brand Commitments

The user likes clear graphic compositions with color drawn from the website. The user rejected distressed print illustrations and overly minimal white title cards. The selected development-log design is original A: an editorial latest-entry feature and a chronological archive. Article covers share the approved forest, cream, sage, and coral graphic style. Covers contain the visual only, without headline or subtitle panels. Show the real article title once outside the image.
