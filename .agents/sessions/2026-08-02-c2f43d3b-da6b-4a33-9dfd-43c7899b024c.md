# Stretching image surface correction

## Context

The stretching tool's recent-routine card allowed a portrait exercise asset to determine the desktop grid row height. At a 1083 by 987 viewport, the card measured 703 pixels tall and displaced the rest of the page.

## User correction

"this aspect ratio is more of an ASS pect ratio ..."

## Resolution

Bound the recent-routine media surface to 384 pixels at desktop widths and a 4:3 surface on mobile. Changed the card presentation to an intentional `object-cover` crop with a 54% vertical focal point. Browser verification measured the desktop card at 386 pixels tall, confirmed a 4:3 mobile surface at 390 by 844, and found no horizontal overflow.
